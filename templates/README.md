# Outreach Templates

Ready-to-use email templates for reaching out to companies from the [Career Launcher](../README.md) list.

**Before you send:**
- Replace every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder with your own details.
- Personalize the "why this company" line — generic emails get ignored.
- Attach your CV/résumé as a PDF.
- Send **one** thoughtful email per company. No bulk sending. Respect the ethical guidelines in the main README.

| Template | Use it when |
|----------|-------------|
| [spontaneous-application.md](spontaneous-application.md) | No open role is posted, but you want to introduce yourself |
| [internship-application.md](internship-application.md) | You're a student seeking an internship |
| [follow-up.md](follow-up.md) | It's been ~1 week and you haven't heard back |
